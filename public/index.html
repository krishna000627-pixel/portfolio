01

QuestBlock

v285

Kotlin, Compose

Built for my own Class 12 preparation. Study apps earn coins, fun apps cost coins, and the app watches which is which.

Quests
Daily, weekly, one-time, focus, routine and challenge types. Finishing one pays 10, 25 or 50 coins and XP, and a streak adds a bonus every day.
Study pays
Time spent in study apps earns coins, about 1 coin for every 5 minutes.
Vault
Fun apps stay locked. I pay coins for 15 minutes up to 10 hours, a live countdown shows what is left, and the app closes when time is up.
Focus
A timer ring and a strict mode that cannot be abandoned. Before a session I choose which apps I am allowed to use.
Study tracker
Chapters for every subject, written from my own Class 12 guide, plus marks and attendance for school and coaching.
Player hub
Ranks, seasons, a discipline score, attributes from finished quests, badges and a weekly review.
Usage stats
Daily screen time split into study, coding, neutral and fun. It lands within 15 to 20 minutes of Digital Wellbeing.
Rules
Everything stricter is an optional switch, like blocking Shorts after the fun limit. Nothing turns on by itself.
Reliability
Android kills background apps, so there is a battery and autostart guide per phone brand, a self test and a debug log.
Themes
Dozens of themes, each with its own effects, fonts and app icon. One is a Termux-style terminal I built myself.

Good to know. Needs accessibility and usage access, because that is how it knows which app is open.

02

App Hub

Kotlin, Compose, Room

Shipping an update used to mean unzipping and typing commands. Now it takes a few taps, and the phone does the rest.

Dashboard
Every app on one screen: passing, failing, waiting to push. Each card has Build and Deploy.
Deploy
Pick the zip. App Hub checks its name and version, shows what changed, then pushes only the changed files as one commit.
Mistake guards
A warning if the zip belongs to another app, and another if it is older than what is on GitHub.
Builds
Every GitHub Actions run with its steps, and a link to the full log.
Releases and install
Each build’s APK with its size. Install the latest, or go back to the previous one. It says Install or Update depending on what is on the phone.
Files
Browse and search the repo, open a file, delete one with a single commit. Undo last push is one tap.
Prompt Maker
Writes the prompt I give Claude: new app, fix error, add feature, refactor. The latest build error is added automatically.
Signing
Creates the keystore, sets the four GitHub secrets, and keeps a backup so updates always use the same key.
Always fresh
Refreshes every 3 or 10 seconds, notifies when a build finishes, and can download new APKs in the background on Wi-Fi.
Beyond apps
Also runs website projects, like this portfolio, and logs in to GitHub, Vercel and Supabase.

Good to know. The backup file contains signing keys, so it has to stay private.

BgBrowser

v11

Kotlin, WebView

Made for reading web novels with the site’s own read-aloud voice while the phone is locked.

Keeps running
A foreground service with a media session, a wake lock and a Wi-Fi lock, so Android leaves the page alone.
Pause that remembers
Pause holds the voice at the current word and Play carries on from there. Works from the notification or a headset button.
Sleep timer
Stops everything after a set time, for falling asleep halfway through a chapter.
Next page
A button that jumps to the next chapter, with your own CSS selector for stubborn sites.
Native voice bridge
Replaces the page’s speech with Android’s own text-to-speech. Experimental, and it can be switched off.
Tor tabs
Three security levels, tracker and ad blocking, link cleaning and a separate circuit per site.
Looks
AMOLED black or light, accent colours, address bar on top or at the bottom for one-hand use.
Setup help
Launch on boot, restore tabs, and a checklist for battery and notification settings.

Good to know. Swiping the app out of recents kills the page. Tor tabs are weaker than Tor Browser, and the app says so.

Videowall

v30

OpenGL

Live wallpapers from videos, GIFs and plain images, kept in one library.

Library
A grid with search, sort, favorites only and remove duplicates.
Set as wallpaper
Preview full screen, choose loop mode, export, then set.
Shuffle
Change the wallpaper on every unlock, or only among starred ones.
Controls
Double tap to pause, audio on or off, fit or crop scaling for video, GIF and images.
Battery
Playing off screen is optional, the decoder is released after 20 seconds away from home, and a freeze slider cuts it further.
Looks
Light or dark, five accent colours, and a choice of grid columns.

Good to know. After the decoder is released the wallpaper may take a moment to reload when you come back.

Aria Reply

Kotlin, Groq, Gemini

I cannot always reply right away, so Aria does it, in Hinglish, and says it is an AI.

Auto-replies
Reads WhatsApp notifications and answers, with every reply marked as automated.
Introduction
If a chat has been quiet for a while, Aria opens by saying it is my AI agent.
Follow-ups
Spots promises, like confirming an arrival time, and keeps a reminder.
Routine
I tell it when I am in college, asleep or at the gym, so it knows when to say I am busy.
Filters
Reply to everyone, a blocklist or a whitelist. Groups are off by default.
Models
Groq or Gemini, with my own system prompt, a reply style and a 2 to 5 second delay.
Notifications
Can swap WhatsApp’s notifications for one Aria summary.
Tools
An API Lab to test the model, a system test, and a log of everything captured, replied to or skipped.

Good to know. Needs notification access and unrestricted battery to reply while the phone is idle.

GScholar

v8

Android

Google Scholar without the clutter, with a library and alerts built in.

Explore

Results

Library

Alerts

Camouflage

Android

Covers the apps I choose with a fake screen, and hides private photos and videos.

Cover apps
A blocked app shows a fake screen instead of opening.
Disguises
Not supported, keeps stopping, not available in your region, can’t open, or under development.
Hidden media
Photos and videos kept behind a PIN, with a decoy PIN as well.
Secret unlocks
Top and bottom holds, volume codes, corner taps and a two-finger hold.
Unlock log
Every attempt to open a covered app is recorded.
Quick tile
Switch it on from the notification panel.
Security
Screenshot blocking, themes and an editable alert text.

Good to know. Needs the accessibility service and all-files access.
